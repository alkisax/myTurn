Delete Account – MyTurn

MyTurn allows registered users to access organization and queue management features.

Delete your account from the app

Where account deletion is available inside MyTurn, you can delete your account directly from the application:

  • Open MyTurn
  • Go to your account or settings page
  • Select "Delete Account"
  • Confirm the deletion

What happens when your account is deleted

Account deletion permanently removes your MyTurn account from the service.

Depending on your role, associated organization data may also be deleted where applicable. Staff accounts may also be removed by an administrator of the organization they belong to.

Some limited technical or security records may be retained temporarily where necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
