Delete Account – Noter

This app allows users to create accounts and store notes locally and on a remote server.

You can delete your account directly from inside the app:

  • Go to Settings
  • Tap "Delete Account"
  • Confirm the action

When you delete your account:

  • Your account is permanently removed via the backend API
  • Your associated data (notes stored on the server) is deleted

Some minimal technical logs may be retained temporarily for security purposes.

If you experience any issues, you can request account deletion by contacting: alkisax@gmail.com
